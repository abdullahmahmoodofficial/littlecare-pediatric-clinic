About LittleCare

A children's clinic built around one idea: children get better care when nobody is in a rush.

WHO WE ARE

Calm rooms, honest advice

LittleCare opened in Johar Town, Lahore, for parents who wanted something simple: a doctor who listens, explains in plain words, and never rushes a child out of the room. Visits last about 30 minutes — long enough for real questions.

A parent or guardian stays in the room for the whole visit. Prices are listed openly. If a test or medicine is not needed, we say so.

What parents ask us most

My child's vaccine is late. Is it a problem?

Usually not. Most missed doses can be caught up safely — book a visit and we will make a catch-up plan.

Do you treat children with special needs?

Yes. Tell us in the booking notes so we can allow extra time and a quieter room.

Can I get advice on WhatsApp first?

For simple questions, yes — message the clinic number and we will reply during clinic hours. Anything urgent should be a visit or an emergency service.
