Good to know before you visit

Honest answers to the questions every parent asks first.

Can I stay with my child during the visit?

Yes, always. A parent or guardian stays in the room for the whole visit — children are never examined alone.

Do you speak Urdu?

Yes. Visits are available in Urdu and English, and advice is explained in plain words, never medical jargon.

How much will the visit cost?

Consultations start at Rs. 1,500 and every service price is listed openly on the services page. Vaccine prices depend on the brand and are confirmed before the visit.

What should I bring?

Your child's vaccination card, any previous reports or medicine lists, and a note of your questions. First visit? Just bring your child — we start from scratch.
